## Brand & Style
This design system embodies soulful slow travel, eco-luxury retreat hospitality, and indigenous craftsmanship rooted in the coastal mangroves and sacred groves of Abéné, Casamance. It communicates an unhurried, grounded luxury—where time dilates, tactile organic textures replace glossy tech motifs, and refined editorial layouts honor cultural heritage and environmental stewardship.

The target audience comprises discerning cultural travelers, creative seekers, and eco-conscious wanderers seeking sanctuary from high-velocity urban life. The visual language blends:
- **Warm Editorial Minimalism:** Airy, breathing compositions balanced with deliberate asymmetric tensions that mirror artisan-built dwellings and open-air verandas.
- **Organic Earthiness & Tactility:** Sun-bleached linen canvases, warm earthen pigments, and delicate stone-toned delineations.
- **Craftsmanship & Reverence:** Traditional typographic nuance using classic transitional serifs alongside geometric sans-serifs, complemented by subtle diamond-motif dividers inspired by local woven reeds and adobe carvings.

## Colors
The color hierarchy is derived directly from the Casamance landscape—sun-baked laterite soil, sacred baobab canopies, sun-dried sand, and deep roasted coffee beans.

- **Primary (`#B85D3B` Laterite Terracotta):** Reserved for primary calls-to-action, key conversion drivers, active state indicators, and signature interactive gestures.
- **Secondary (`#3F5443` Baobab Olive Green):** Botanical accents, environmental tags, sustainability badges, and subtle hover micro-interactions.
- **Tertiary (`#C4B39A` Warm Stone):** Subtle 1px borders, ornamental diamond nodes, and decorative hairline dividers.
- **Neutral (`#382112` Deep Espresso Brown):** Primary typographic ink, high-emphasis text, and dark structural contours.
- **Neutral Subtle (`#735C49` Muted Bark Brown):** Secondary copy, captions, timestamps, and supporting metadata.
- **Backgrounds:** The primary viewport uses Warm Sand (`#EFE6D8`), alternating with elevated Raw Linen surfaces (`#F7F2EA`) for cards, drawers, and nested containers. Avoid stark synthetic pure white (`#FFFFFF`) entirely.

## Typography
The typographic architecture pairs the literary, historic gravitas of EB Garamond with the crisp, modern legibility of Plus Jakarta Sans. 

- **Editorial Headings (`EB Garamond`):** Headlines and titles project an unhurried literary feel. Use `display` and `headline-lg` in SemiBold (600) for resonant hero sections and chapter introductions. Use `headline-md` and `headline-sm` in Regular (400) or Italic for evocative subheaders and narrative pull quotes.
- **Eyebrows & Navigation (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Render in uppercase with strict `0.2em` letter-spacing (tracking-widest). This brings balance to large serif titles and reinforces an understated architectural rhythm.
- **Body & Reading Rhythm (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Use Light (300) and Regular (400) weights with a loose line-height of `1.65` across narrative copy. Paragraphs must avoid cramped settings to promote a peaceful, deliberate reading experience.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout strategy prioritizes generous negative space, intentional asymmetry, and a relaxed cadence reminiscent of an independent luxury travel journal.

- **Grid Architecture:** 
  - **Desktop (12 columns):** 64px margins with a fluid inner container capped at 1440px max width. Allows for staggered multi-column image groupings, off-center focal cards, and side-rail editorial quotes.
  - **Tablet (8 columns):** 40px margins with flexible column pairs.
  - **Mobile (4 columns):** 20px margins, collapsing multi-column editorial modules into vertical single-column stacks with edge-to-edge full-bleed imagery.
- **Rhythm & White Space:** Vertical spacing between major thematic chapters expands generously to `space-xl` (64px) and custom section increments (96px–120px on desktop) to let photography and narratives breathe naturally.
- **Image Compositions:** Embrace non-uniform aspect ratios (e.g., pairing a 3:4 portrait crop beside a 16:9 landscape vignette) with subtle vertical offsets to evoke hand-placed artisan documentation.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is communicated through surface tone stacking and artisan line work rather than industrial drop shadows.

- **Tonal Layers:** The base canvas sits at `#EFE6D8` (Warm Sand). Elevated modules, booking sheets, and inspection overlays lift subtly using `#F7F2EA` (Raw Linen), creating tactile separation like sheets of handmade cotton rag paper resting on a wooden table.
- **Shadowless Elegance:** Avoid synthetic dark drop shadows. If an element requires floating elevation (e.g., sticky booking bars or modal panels), employ a faint, warm-toned ambient veil: `0 16px 36px -12px rgba(56, 33, 18, 0.08)`.
- **Low-Contrast Structural Borders:** Delimit cards, calendar cells, and navigation segments with 1px solid lines using `#C4B39A` (Warm Stone).
- **Artisan Dividers:** Break major narrative sequences with a continuous horizontal 1px line punctuated at the center by a rotated 4px × 4px diamond accent (`#C4B39A`), grounding the technical layout in handcrafted motif detailing.

## Shapes
Geometry is tailored, structured, and disciplined. Soft, organic edges (`roundedness: 1`) provide a subtle human touch without appearing child-like or overly pill-shaped.

- **Base Radius (0.25rem / 4px):** Applied consistently to primary buttons, interactive badge pills, date pickers, and input fields. This slight softening mimics traditional bookbinding trim and hand-carved woodblocks.
- **Containers & Editorial Cards:** Maintain either razor-sharp (0px) edges with 1px stone borders or subtle 4px corner radii for modals and secondary trays.
- **Image Frames:** Photographic elements keep clean right angles (0px) or gentle 4px bounds, honoring full architectural and natural landscape framing without artificial bubbles.

## Components

- **Buttons:**
  - **Primary CTA:** Surface `#B85D3B` (Laterite Terracotta) with `#F7F2EA` (Raw Linen) label, 4px border radius. Internal padding: `14px 28px`. Typography: `label-md` uppercase with `0.1em` letter spacing. On hover, deepen tone toward `#9E4A2C`.
  - **Secondary CTA:** Surface transparent with a 1px border in `#735C49`. Text `#382112`. On hover, fills with `#EFE6D8` and transitions border to `#382112`.
  - **Tertiary / Text Link:** Text `#382112` with a persistent bottom 1px line in `#B85D3B` offset by 4px, accompanied by a trailing fine serif arrow (`→`).

- **Eyebrows & Chapter Dividers:**
  - Small uppercase tags using `eyebrow` styling in `#735C49`.
  - Section dividers use a 1px solid line in `#C4B39A` with a centered 45-degree diamond symbol (`◆`).

- **Input Fields & Date Pickers:**
  - Background `#F7F2EA` (Raw Linen), border 1px solid `#C4B39A`, 4px corner radius.
  - Text rendered in `#382112` with placeholder text in `#735C49` at 60% opacity.
  - Active/Focus state: border turns `#382112` with zero synthetic glow, maintaining crisp tactile definition.

- **Editorial Cards:**
  - Composed with `#F7F2EA` background, bounded by a 1px `#C4B39A` outline. Padding: `24px` to `32px`.
  - Images within cards sit flush to top/sides with zero internal radius, separated from copy by a hairline divider.

- **Badges & Chips:**
  - Ecological/wellness pills use `#3F5443` at 10% opacity for the background with solid `#3F5443` text, 4px border radius, and compact padding (`4px 10px`).

- **Checkboxes & Radios:**
  - 18px × 18px square (checkbox) or circle (radio) with a 1px `#C4B39A` border on `#F7F2EA`. When selected, filled with `#3F5443` or `#B85D3B` with an off-white inner mark.

- **Editorial Quote Modules:**
  - Left-aligned or centered pull-quotes using `headline-md` italic (`EB Garamond`) in `#382112`, framed above and below by subtle stone lines with center diamond marks.
